How it works

Quick quizzes, exciting challenges

A game is ten questions long – just right for a bus ride or a coffee break. Pick a topic and a hero, answer, collect gold and stars, and level up!

1

Pick a topic and a hero

History, geography, literature, nature, science, art, entertainment or sport – each one has its own hero, and you are the one who trains them.

2

Answer the questions

Ten questions, four options each, and a ticking clock. If you get stuck, the helpers step in: the halver, the freeze, the shield or a topic swap.

3

Level up your heroes

Every correct answer earns gold, stars and XP. Stars level up your heroes, and higher levels bring tougher questions and bigger rewards.

Game modes

A reason to come back every day

The quiz is only the beginning. Daily and weekly goals, mini quests on niche topics and leaderboards keep the game moving.

Daily challenges

Fresh tasks every day: play a few rounds, answer correctly in a given topic, collect stars. Complete them and extra gold lands in your pocket.

Mini quests

Narrow, exciting topics – World War II, astronomy, Formula 1 or Renaissance art, for example. Tickets get you in, and a separate leaderboard shows how you measure up.

Leaderboards and schools

A weekly XP leaderboard for players, and a separate one for schools: tell us where you study, and every correct answer counts for your school as well. Game Center and Google Play Games supported.

Weekly rewards

The more correct answers you give during the week, the further you climb the weekly reward ladder: gold, diamonds, helpers and phoenixes that revive an exhausted hero.

Who it's for

Not just for kids

TinyMinds is for anyone who likes a battle of wits: students revising the fun way, adults who love a good pub quiz, and grandparents keen to show how much they know. Three difficulty levels make sure it is never too easy – or too hard – for anyone.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Is the game really free?

Yes. TinyMinds is free to download and the whole game can be played without buying anything. The app contains ads, and if you choose to watch a rewarded video you get extra gold, tickets or a phoenix. Supporter packs and subscriptions are optional.

What age is it for?

Everyone. The store rating is 4+ (App Store) and PEGI 3 (Google Play), and the questions come in three difficulty levels, so there is something for everyone from primary school pupils to grandparents.

Do I need to sign up?

No. On first launch you only pick a player name and, if you like, a school. Your progress is tied to your device and to your Game Center or Google Play Games account, so it can be restored after a reinstall.

Which languages can I play in?

English and Hungarian. You choose the language on first launch and can switch it any time in the settings – the questions are available in both languages.

How do I restore my purchases or delete my account?

Both options are in the app on the Settings screen: “Restore purchases” and “Delete account”. Deleting your account permanently removes all data associated with it. You can find more help on the support page.

I found a bug or have a question. Where can I write?

Email us at tinymindsquiz@gmail.com and we will get back to you as soon as we can.
